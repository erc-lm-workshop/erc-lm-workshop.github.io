ICASSP 2027 Workshop Proposal

Explainability, Robustness, and Controllability of Language Modeling for Speech and Audio

Explainability, robustness, and controllability for audio and speech language models.

Call for papers Proposal PDF

Scope

Language modeling for speech and audio

Explainability

Methods and studies that help explain how audio and speech language models make decisions, including post-hoc interpretability, attribution, reasoning analyses, and faithfulness.

Robustness

Work on noise, adversarial inputs, prompt injections, hallucinations, temporal shifts, safety, security, and behavior under distribution shift.

Controllability

Architectures and representations that enable control over speech, music, and audio generation or editing while preserving unrelated content.

Call for Papers

Submissions

We plan to invite four-page submissions in the ICASSP format, with an optional appendix. Submissions will be double blind and peer reviewed on OpenReview.
